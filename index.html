What we do

Our services

We design and build complete technological solutions — from the first schematic to the cloud dashboard. No off-the-shelf products, everything custom.

Embedded Systems

Custom firmware and real-time software for microcontrollers and microprocessors (STM32, ESP32, NRF52, RISC-V, and more). From bare metal to RTOS.

IoT & LoRa

Connected device networks, LoRaWAN architectures, MQTT gateways, and end-to-end IoT solutions designed for reliability and low power consumption.

Custom Hardware

PCB design, schematic capture, component selection, and prototype-to-production support. We design the exact hardware your project needs.

Cloud Software

Backend APIs, real-time dashboards, data pipelines, and cloud infrastructure tailored to your IoT ecosystem or industrial application.

Sensing & Instrumentation

Custom sensor integration, signal conditioning, ADC design, and data acquisition systems for industrial, environmental, and scientific applications.

Technical Consulting

Technology assessment, architecture review, feasibility studies, and technical due diligence. We help you make the right technical decisions from day one.

Our process

How we work

A clear process, no surprises. We work closely with you at every step.

01

We understand the problem

We start with a deep technical conversation. We listen to your challenge, ask the right questions, and make sure we fully understand what you need before we propose anything.

02

We design the solution

We propose a concrete technical plan: architecture, technologies, timeline, and cost. You validate every decision before we write a single line of code or draw a trace.

03

We build it

We develop hardware and software in parallel, with regular updates and live demos. You always know the current state of the project — no black boxes.

04

We deliver & support

Delivery includes full documentation, source files, and a transition period. We don't just hand it over — we make sure it works in your real environment.
