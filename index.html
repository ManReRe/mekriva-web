Our process

How we work

A clear process, no surprises. We work closely with you at every step.

01

We understand the problem

We start with a deep technical conversation. We listen to your challenge, ask the right questions, and make sure we fully understand what you need before we propose anything.

02

We design the solution

We propose a concrete technical plan: architecture, technologies, timeline, and cost. You validate every decision before we write a single line of code or draw a trace.

03

We build it

We develop hardware and software in parallel, with regular updates and live demos. You always know the current state of the project — no black boxes.

04

We deliver & support

Delivery includes full documentation, source files, and a transition period. We don't just hand it over — we make sure it works in your real environment.

The team

Small team,
big expertise.

We are a small team of engineers with over a decade of experience building real technology products. Our work spans the full technical spectrum: from circuit design and the firmware running on the microcontroller, to the APIs and cloud infrastructure that make data actionable. The team that talks to you is the same team that designs, codes, and delivers — no outsourcing, no hand-offs.

This full-stack capability — hardware, firmware, and cloud under one roof — is what sets us apart. In IoT and industrial electronics, problems rarely respect layer boundaries. Having the same engineers thinking about the circuit and the data architecture eliminates friction, accelerates development, and produces more coherent solutions.
